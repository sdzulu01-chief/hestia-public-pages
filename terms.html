Hestia Calendar DEV — Terms of Use

Hestia Calendar DEV is a private household calendar viewer developed by Hermosillo Infrastructure Group. It is intended for approved household use, not public account registration or a general scheduling service.

Authorized use

Connect only an account and shared calendar you are authorized to use. Displayed shared events can be visible to eligible human members of the bound household. Keep sensitive information out of shared event titles unless it is appropriate for those members to see it.

Read-only convenience

Hestia shows the day's schedule and does not create or change events, send reminders or undertake household actions. Continue using Google Calendar to manage your schedule.

Availability and accuracy

The application is experimental. Google access can fail or be revoked, and displayed information may become outdated. Confirm important appointments in Google Calendar. Hestia is not an emergency, safety or guaranteed reminder service.

Privacy and disconnection

The privacy notice explains the feature's information handling and how to disconnect. Contact the household administrator for support.
